The problem

Google saw the publishing spike. It didn't see anything behind it.

Twenty posts a month is a signal. When none of them attract a single link, mention, or citation, it's the wrong signal. A batch of content that nobody in the world references reads as low-value at best and manufactured at worst. Publishing more of it doesn't fix the pattern. It confirms it.

0 REFERRING DOMAINS

You fired the agency, wired up the AI stack, and tripled output. Every post landed with no outside link, no mention, no citation.

FLAT

Traffic didn't move. Not down — flat, which is worse, because it means the work is invisible rather than wrong.

NOT CITED

AI answer engines quote sources that other sources reference. Content nothing points at doesn't get pulled into answers either. You're now invisible in two places instead of one.

SUNK

At even 3 hours of review per post, 40 invisible posts is a full month of your working life spent on assets nobody has ever seen.

Objections

The questions people actually ask

Isn't this just link building with extra steps?

No, and the difference is the part that matters. Link building buys placement on a site that sells placement. We pitch editors and hosts who decide independently whether your angle is worth their audience, and they say no fairly often. That's why the links hold value and why nobody gets a penalty. If we could just buy them, we'd charge you less and sleep worse.

My content is AI-assisted. Will hosts care?

Hosts book people, not posts. Your post is the evidence that you have something specific to say — the cost breakdown, the local data, the ten years of cases. Nobody has ever asked us which tool drafted a blog post. They ask whether the guest can hold a 40-minute conversation. If your posts are entirely unedited output with no expertise behind them, the audit will say so and we'll tell you not to buy placements yet.

Do you guarantee placements?

No. Anyone who guarantees editorial placement is either buying it or lying. What we guarantee is that you only pay for placements that actually publish. If we pitch for three months and land nothing, you've spent $200 and we've spent our time.

$200 for an audit I could run myself in Ahrefs?

You could pull referring domains yourself, and if you already do, you may not need the audit. What the tools stop short of is the second half: which of your posts are worth pitching, which specific hosts and editors have a gap those posts fill, and what the angle is. BuzzSumo tells you what's working for other people. We tell you what to do on Tuesday.

How long until something lands?

First pitches go out within a week of submission. Podcast bookings typically confirm in 2–5 weeks and record 3–8 weeks out. Newsletter mentions can land in days. Expert quotes depend on who's filing that week. Anyone quoting you a faster number is describing paid placement.

I'm a lawyer / dentist. Is any of this a compliance problem?

It's the reason clients in regulated fields use us. There's no payment to any publisher, no undisclosed consideration, and no advertorial. You're a source and a guest. We'll supply documentation of the placement process if your bar association or compliance officer wants it. We're not your compliance advisor — run it past yours — but this is the version designed to survive that conversation.

What if the audit says my content is fine?

Then you've learned something worth more than $200, and we refund it. If fewer than half your posts come back invisible, distribution isn't your bottleneck and we'll say so in writing rather than selling you placements you don't need.
